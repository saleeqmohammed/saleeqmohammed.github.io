Profile & Philosophy

The "Engineer Turned Researcher" Story

Combining rigorous academic research with production-grade engineering discipline to deliver robotic autonomy that performs reliably in the physical world.

I am a robotics and AI researcher currently pursuing my doctorate as a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions (MSCA) Doctoral Candidate at Universität Bremen within the SECORO Group (Software Engineering for Cognitive Robotics) on the EU CAVECORE project.

Before my PhD, I worked as a Research Associate at the University of Birmingham on the Horizon EU / UKRI CONVINCE Project, engineering hierarchical deliberation frameworks combining Behavior Trees (py_trees_ros), symbolic Task and Motion Planning (PDDLStream), and physical manipulation on Universal Robots UR10e and Kinova Gen3 arms.

My industrial roots include working as an EDU System Integration Engineer for Jaguar Land Rover (optimizing electric powertrains through automated simulation pipelines) and Tata Elxsi, building upon an MSc in Robotics (Distinction) from University of Birmingham and a B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering from IIT Tirupati.
